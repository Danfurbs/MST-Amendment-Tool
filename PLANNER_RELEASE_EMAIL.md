Subject: MST Amendment Tool — updates for next week's release

Hi all,

An updated version of the MST Amendment Tool will be available from [release date] at [tool link]. Please use Microsoft Edge.

The recent changes include:

- **Open work-order warnings.** A muted red calendar border and WO count identify MSTs with open work orders recorded in the download. The editor shows a pale rose warning alongside any TV information. Counts are also visible in list and bulk views.
- **Confirmation before scheduling changes.** If the snapshot records open work orders, changing dates or frequency requires you to confirm that you have checked Ellipse and are satisfied the changes will not cause an issue. This applies to editor saves, calendar dragging and bulk changes. Cancelling leaves the proposed amendments unapplied.
- **Deactivation guidance.** Open work orders remain active even when their MST is deactivated. Arrange to close them, or cancel them if the work is not complete. Removing a newly created MST removes its queued creation from the batch.
- **Stronger validation.** Frequencies must be whole numbers greater than zero; invalid frequencies appear in the MST error checks. Work-group codes must be exactly seven characters.
- **Clearer scheduling advice.** Amendment reviews explain scheduling warnings and show the proposed changes before you apply them.
- **Improved review and export.** The review includes amendments, new MSTs and deactivations, with clearer presentation and pagination for larger batches. Export handling and batch resumption have received additional checks.
- **More flexible creation.** Multiple MSTs can use the same equipment and standard job where required.
- **Usability improvements.** Updated presentation, improved large-download/calendar performance, resource-chart corrections and warnings about unexported work.
- **Updated guided tour.** Includes a fictional MST with open work orders and a practice acknowledgement explaining what planners need to check.

The work-order count is a weekly snapshot, not a live Ellipse check. A recorded count may have changed since download. Please use the latest approved extract and check the current position in Ellipse before making scheduling changes.

Review your batch before exporting, and retain the exported workbook so you can resume your work. Export before closing or replacing a session.
